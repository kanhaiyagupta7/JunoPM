## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Jira prioritization
- **Value proposition:** Turn a backlog or feature-request list into a ranked recommendation tied to stated business goals

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy  | Minimum 5 features  in a single category for it to be valid | 1 to 5 | less that 5 issues (makes the category invalid) | _ | Issue 1 - 5 or more with recommendations  |
| Compliance (deal-breaker) | Does each Priority  show recommendation attached as per required  | 1 to 5 |  recommend  feature is aligned and ticket created in Jira  | _ |  recommend  feature is not aligned and need revisit   |
| Clarity of verbiage | Will an exec understand the description & priorities | 1 to 5 | Will an exec understand the description & priorities | does the features/ story aligned with goal or need some alignment  | The issue is explained including end user pain point |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 2 -3 answers a day
- **Who scores them with you:** Cross functional team( Me, Senior PM, Architect and Lead)
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** We put our thought and business value based on input and align with value generate , discuss and agree based on the requirements/ Criteria

## Pass bar

All Yes answers and rating of 4 where relevant

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Compliance: any score of 1
- A single hallucination
