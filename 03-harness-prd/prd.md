## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Sources (all the access  must be authorized to the analyst and scoped to the claim/member):

PBM claims adjudication platform: 
claim ID, transaction status, reject code, NDC, quantity, days supply, fill date, date of service, pharmacy identifier, prior transaction references.
Member eligibility platform: coverage status and effective/termination dates for the date of service.

Benefit configuration system: plan/group identifiers, applicable benefit rules, refill-too-soon threshold, quantity limits, and relevant overrides.
Formulary management system: drug coverage status, tier, restrictions, and effective-dated formulary version.
Prior authorization platform: authorization status, effective dates, expiration, and drug/member match.
Pharmacy network system: pharmacy participation status on the date of service, when relevant.

Approved PBM policy/reject-code knowledge base: versioned reject-code definitions and operational guidance. 
Retrieved passages must retain document ID, version, effective date, and section/clause.

Excluded: unrelated member claims,  other employer groups or plan configurations

**How fresh must it be**

Claim status, eligibility, Prior authorization status, and pharmacy network status are retrieved at investigation time. 
Benefit and formulary rules must be selected for the relevant plan and date of service using effective-dated configuration. 
Policy content  to the event being investigated.

Unreachable source: if a source is unavailable, the agent identifies the missing source, does not infer the missing fact, and returns an incomplete investigation for human review.
